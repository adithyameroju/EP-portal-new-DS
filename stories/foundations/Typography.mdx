# Typography

From `.claude/specs/foundations/typography.md`: "Compass uses **Euclid Circular B** as its sole typeface — across sans, serif, and mono slots."

## Font family

The faces load via `app/globals.css` → `app/fonts.css` (`@font-face "Euclid Circular B"`), and the family tokens come from the token package's `@theme` block.

## Font weights

The self-hosted faces registered in `app/fonts.css` cover weights 300–700 (plus italics); spec-listed weights outside that range are browser-synthesized.
