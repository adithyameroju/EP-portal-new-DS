## Documented in spec, not present in the published package

The spec's alpha tokens (`alpha-5` … `alpha-90`) and `--ring-offset` are not defined in `@acko/enterprise-tokens@1.0.0` `globals.css`, so they are not rendered here — this gap is part of the pending FE-dev token verification.
